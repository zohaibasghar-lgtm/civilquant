# Play Store listing draft — CivilQuant

**App name:** CivilQuant: Steel BBS & Estimation

**Short description:** Calculate steel weights, BBS lengths and site quantities. Export field measurement sheets.

**Full description:**
CivilQuant helps civil engineers, site supervisors and quantity surveyors prepare day-to-day estimates. Calculate reinforcing steel weight, bar cutting lengths, concrete and excavation volumes, plaster and formwork areas, flooring, masonry and road quantities. Capture site photos, scan visible drawing text offline with on-device OCR and export a clear measurement sheet as PDF. Local calculators are available without creating an account. All outputs are estimates that must be verified against drawings, construction standards and actual measurements.

Cloud AI drawing analysis and Pro subscriptions are planned. Do not list them as available until accounts and backend are active.

**Category:** Productivity / Tools.

**Audience:** Construction professionals and adult students.

**Permissions:** Camera for explicit image capture; selected photos/PDF files only. Confirm AdMob privacy and all final Play Console Data Safety answers after production integration is verified.

**Keywords (for product research, not keyword stuffing):** civil engineering, bar bending schedule, rebar calculator, steel weight, construction estimation, concrete volume, quantity surveying, measurement sheets.
